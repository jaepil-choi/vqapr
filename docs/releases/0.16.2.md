# vqapr 0.16.2 — an account may borrow, and long research runs get faster

Released 2026-09-15 from `develop`. Two pieces of work: the borrowing-account campaign, records
`288`–`290`, from the owner's question "the academic exchange may hold more than 100% long — how do
we allow it?", and the performance records `285`–`287` merged after 0.16.1. A patch: no registered
workspace, run record or author import changes, and a run that does not declare borrowing computes
and records exactly what it did.

## An account may borrow (records `288`–`290`)

- **The declaration.** `initial_account.cash_mode: BORROWING` lets the account's cash go below zero.
  `FUNDED` is the default and is never written, so an existing run's stored declaration, identity
  and `run.json` are unchanged. `CashMode` is exported from `vqapr.public`.
- **The limit is the strategy's budget.** `cash_lower` is a share of NAV:
  `Rebalance.signed({"A": 1, "B": 1}, gross=2)` is 200% long with cash at -1, one NAV borrowed. The
  account keeps no amount of its own.
- **The planner reads the same fact.** A funded account's buys are still cut to the cash on hand. A
  borrowing account's are not, so the leverage the budget asked for is the leverage that fills.
- **Borrowed cash is free.** No interest, no margin, no forced sale. `run.json` says
  `initial_account.cash_mode: borrowing`, and the skills say to report it. For a financing cost,
  keep `FUNDED`, declare the account `SIGNED`, and short a derived unit-price asset that grows at
  the borrowing rate.
- **A book worth nothing stops with what happened.** At NAV ≤ 0 the next order plan stops with
  "the book is worth X at this instant … no margin call is modelled", instead of
  `execution_time_nav must be positive`.
- **KRX cannot lend.** The KRX profile cuts every buy to cash (`partial_fills: cash-limited`), so
  `check` and `run` refuse a borrowing account on it: `weights.cash_conflict` (412).
- Architecture §9.3 and §9.4: `cash >= 0` is now the funded account's invariant, not every account's.

## Long research runs get faster (records `285`–`287`)

- Panel evidence is built lazily, and execution reads take up to 1M rows at a time (`285`).
- A DataModel checks its output field names once per batch rather than once per row (`286`).
- The skills show how several strategies reuse one materialized DataModel output (`287`).

## Migrating a 0.16.1 tree

- Nothing to register again.
- A caller that matched `execution_time_nav must be positive` or `fill batch would make cash
  negative` on the exact text: both sentences changed.
- Installed skills describe the old wording. Install them again with `vqapr skill install --force`.
